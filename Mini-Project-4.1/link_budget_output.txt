PART B: 200 sensors, 25 km2 farm, 12-byte reading every 30 min, max distance 8 km, target 5 y on 19000 mAh
Path loss: PL(d) = 91.5 + 10 x 3.5 x log10(d/1 km);  PL(8 km) = 123.11 dB
MCL = Ptx - receiver sensitivity:  NB-IoT 23 - (-129) = 152 dB,  LTE-M (CE Mode B) 23 - (-125) = 148 dB,  LoRaWAN SF12 14 - (-137) = 151 dB

Battery assumptions (representative figures, NOT measurements):
  NB-IoT               TX 110 mA x 8.00 s; sleep 5 uA; repetition-based coverage enhancement, PSM sleep
  LTE-M (CE Mode B)    TX 190 mA x 6.00 s; sleep 8 uA; higher transmit current, shorter on-air time than NB-IoT
  LoRaWAN SF12         TX 44 mA x 1.48 s + RX 12 mA x 0.4 s; sleep 5 uA; time-on-air 1.48 s (Semtech formula), two short RX windows
  5G RedCap            TX 250 mA x 1.50 s; sleep 15 uA; representative: 20 MHz carrier, noise figure 7 dB, low-SNR MCS; eDRX sleep
  Cycles per year = 17520; self-discharge 1 %/year = 190 mAh/year
